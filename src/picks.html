How to read the stars

★ Easy to learn: how quickly you'll go from opening the box to using it on your own, setup included.

★ Can do: how many of ten everyday jobs it handles. Only do-it-all devices get this one.

How we rate

Easy to learn

How long from opening the box until you can do the main job on your own? Setup counts, so a pick that needs a helper to get going earns fewer stars.

  • ★★★★★ The first day, no lesson needed
  • ★★★★★ After one short lesson
  • ★★★★★ After about a week of use
  • ★★★★★ After several weeks of practice
  • ★★★★★ Needs ongoing help

Can do

How many of ten everyday jobs can it handle? Calls, texts, video calls, photos, email, the web, shopping and banking, health apps, entertainment, and reminders. A job it only partly does counts as half.

  • ★★★★★ 9 to 10 jobs
  • ★★★★★ 7 to 8 jobs
  • ★★★★★ 5 to 6 jobs
  • ★★★★★ 3 to 4 jobs
  • ★★★★★ 1 to 2 jobs

A night light that does one job well shouldn't lose stars for it, so single-purpose items only get "Easy to learn."

Our honest judgment, not a lab test.
