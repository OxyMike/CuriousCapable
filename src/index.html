Why Curious & Capable

Technology shouldn't be a source of stress.

Most tech gets taught to older adults one of two ways: too fast, or too patronizing. We do neither. With a background in both healthcare and IT, we understand the human side and the technical side, so help is always compassionate, clear, and genuinely useful.

  • ✓ Patient, jargon-free explanations
  • ✓ Real skills you'll use this week
  • ✓ Flexible: in a group, at home, or online
  • ✓ Every question treated as a good one
